# Two-minute demo script

This is the planned final story. Use it after the complete cross-role journey
exists; do not present Milestone 1 shells as functioning operations.

## Story

1. Start on the public ServiceFlow page and establish BrightHome Services as the
   fictional Bay Area customer. Point out the demo disclosure.
2. As Jamie Chen, choose HVAC repair, a valid address, and an available two-hour
   window. Complete the clearly simulated $49 diagnostic deposit.
3. Enter as administrator Morgan Lee. Find the new confirmed job and assign Alex
   Rivera, showing how ServiceFlow prevents scheduling conflicts.
4. Enter Alex's mobile technician view. Move the job from assigned to en route,
   in progress, and completed; add a completion summary and final price.
5. Return as Jamie. Show the complete status timeline, payment and notification
   history, completion summary, and final invoice.
6. Close with the engineering evidence: server-side authorization, explicit
   state machines, deterministic data, automated tests, audit history, and the
   provider-neutral payment boundary.

The point is one accountable workflow, not a tour of disconnected screens.
